# Blind skim

Perihelion · episode 4 · 7:00

The third beacon orbits a gas giant that could fill the tanks. Reading its depth means transmitting, and this ship does not transmit. The engineer offers another way.

## Scene 1 · Kiln (0:04)

*Open on a hot amber gas giant, small and off centre, growing. The teal relay trace keeps its seven-second count underneath everything. Madrigal is in the drive room, where she has been for 426 days.*

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 426 · 05:40**

> Six days out from the Shallows. Three suits on a rack and a recorder I left where it sat. Nobody has asked me to go back for it. Kiln is ahead, and beacon 3.

**Okafor:** Sublight. Kiln on screen, Captain.

**Pell:** Cloud tops at nine hundred kelvin. It was named honestly.

**Madrigal:** Braking burn. It will cost five points.

**Varga:** We cannot arrive without stopping, Madrigal.

**Madrigal:** I did not object. I priced it.

*Meter: fuel 46% to 41%.*

*The fuel meter drains from 46 to 41 while the planet grows. Nobody speaks. The ship marker settles on Kiln.*

*The amber trace wakes on the signal strip. Achterberg lets it pulse twice before she says the number.*

**Achterberg:** Beacon 3. Aphelion ident. Timing the interval.

**Achterberg:** Forty-four seconds.

**Okafor:** Fourteen fewer.

**Halloran:** Fourteen, between one star and the next.

**Castellanos:** Nobody loses fourteen to bad luck. That is a decision.

**Varga:** Tightbeam, Achterberg. Receive only.

`day 426 · 06:30`

*The viewscreen switches to the beacon wireframe. Two entries print beside it.*

**Achterberg:** Locked. Its memory holds two entries.

**ON SCREEN · entry 1 · Aphelion day 512**

> Fourteen voted to leave with the lander. I let them. A captain who counts her crew each morning has already lost them.

**Castellanos:** I count stores each morning. Stores do not vote.

**Okafor:** She let them go.

**Varga:** She could not have held fourteen. She says so.

**ON SCREEN · entry 2 · Aphelion day 512**

> The giant will fill your tanks. Do not range it. Ranging is answering.

*Hold on the second entry. The fuel meter reads 41 beside it.*

**Okafor:** A fuel stop, with conditions.

**Varga:** Madrigal. I want a fuel state within the hour.

**Madrigal:** You will not like it.

## Scene 2 · The price (1:55)

`day 426 · 08:15`

*Castellanos and Pell walk to the bridge. Five dots there now. The doctor listens from med, Madrigal from the drive room. The viewscreen prints her arithmetic.*

**ON SCREEN · tanks**

> Fuel 41 percent. Transit to Vesper: 33. Remaining on arrival: 8.

**ON SCREEN · return**

> Nearest charted star from Vesper: 38. Shortfall: 30.

**Madrigal:** Enough to reach Vesper. Not enough to leave it for anywhere.

**Varga:** And the giant?

**Madrigal:** A skim fills us. A normal skim ranges the cloud deck twice a second to read depth.

**Castellanos:** That is transmitting.

**Madrigal:** That is how everyone who has done it is still alive.

**Achterberg:** Captain. We have answered once already. What does a second time cost?

**Castellanos:** Ask the Aphelion.

**Pell:** A ranging pulse carries nothing. No names, no words. It is a ruler.

**Okafor:** It counted seven of us from eight tenths of a second.

*Okafor says it to the room. The captain does not look at him. Nothing on the console shows that, so the pause has to.*

**Halloran:** Without ranging, how does anyone know how deep we are?

**Madrigal:** I can fly it blind, on drive pressure alone. The drive feels the gas before the hull does.

**Madrigal:** Note that I think it is a bad idea. Note that I am offering.

**Varga:** Noted. Twice.

**Pell:** I can stand in the drive room with her. A second pair of hands.

**Madrigal:** No. One person in here is a crowd.

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 426 · 09:10**

> The standing order holds: this ship does not transmit. We will skim Kiln without ranging, on Madrigal and her pressure gauges. She has objected and she has volunteered. I have accepted both.

**Castellanos:** Thirty-one crates to tie down. I will count them again after.

**Halloran:** Med is ready. I would like it to stay empty.

*Castellanos walks back to the store and Pell to the lab. Kiln creeps larger. One dot in the drive room, alone, as always.*

## Scene 3 · Blind skim (3:44)

`day 426 · 13:20`

*The console frame turns red. The planet swells until there is no edge left on the viewscreen, only bands. From here the story is two meters: fuel going up, hull going down.*

**Madrigal:** Scoop open. Taking pressure.

*Meter: fuel 41% to 47%.*

**Okafor:** Fuel forty-seven.

**Madrigal:** Deeper.

*Meter: hull 88% to 84%.*

*First jolt. Hull 88 to 84 in less than a second.*

**Achterberg:** Hull eighty-four.

*Meter: fuel 47% to 54%.*

*Meter: hull 84% to 79%.*

*Meter: fuel 54% to 61%.*

*Second jolt. Hull 79, fuel 61. The lines cross somewhere ahead and everyone on the bridge can see where.*

**Varga:** Madrigal. Depth.

**Madrigal:** Unknown. That was the arrangement.

*Meter: hull 79% to 73%.*

*Meter: fuel 61% to 68%.*

**Okafor:** Fuel sixty-eight. Hull seventy-three.

**Varga:** Enough. Bring us up.

**Madrigal:** Ten more points. Then up.

*Meter: hull 73% to 64%.*

*Meter: fuel 68% to 74%.*

*The worst jolt. Hull 73 to 64 at once. The status line names a conduit in the drive room.*

**Varga:** Madrigal?

*No answer. Her dot is still in the drive room. It is not moving, but it never does.*

*Meter: fuel 74% to 78%.*

**Varga:** Madrigal, respond.

*Meter: morale 30% to 26%.*

**Okafor:** Tanks at seventy-eight. The drive is holding trim on its own.

**Varga:** I have the helm. Climbing. Doctor, drive room.

*The doctor runs: med to the drive room in three seconds, the fastest any dot has moved on this ship. The planet begins to fall away.*

**Varga:** Mr Okafor. Go.

**Halloran:** She is breathing. Conduit burn. Help me lift.

*Three dots leave the drive room together, slowly, one of them carried. It is the first time Madrigal has left it. No dialogue until the scene ends.*

*Hold on the deck plan. The drive room is empty. It has not been empty since the ship left dock.*

## Scene 4 · Empty drive room (5:26)

`day 427 · 06:10`

*Day 427. The frame is amber again. Pell walks from the lab to the drive room and Okafor returns to the bridge. Madrigal and the doctor are in med.*

**Halloran:** Conduit burn, left arm and shoulder. She keeps both.

**Halloran:** She set the trim before she went down. Then she went down.

**Castellanos:** Tanks at seventy-eight. I counted twice.

**Achterberg:** Hull at sixty-four. Frames 12 and 31 are braced.

**Madrigal:** Who is in my drive room?

**Varga:** Pell.

**Madrigal:** Tell him not to touch the trim.

**Pell:** I have touched nothing. I am standing in the middle.

*Meter: morale 26% to 36%.*

*Morale climbs from 26 to 36 on the sound of her voice. Nobody remarks on it.*

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 427 · 06:40**

> We took thirty-seven points of fuel from Kiln and paid twenty-four of hull. No pulse left this ship. Madrigal is stable. Her objection is on record. So is my answer.

`day 427 · 22:05`

*22:05. The doctor goes to the mess, as he does. Tonight med is not empty behind him. Achterberg and Castellanos turn in. Captain and first officer have the bridge.*

**Okafor (private):** After Kiln, you said.

**Varga (private):** She nearly died keeping my order. Give me until she is on her feet.

**Okafor (private):** That is the last time I wait.

*The private channel closes. Two dots on the bridge, not speaking. The relay pulses every seven seconds.*

**Varga:** Set course for Vesper, Mr Okafor.

*A dashed course line reaches for Vesper. Kiln falls astern. The doctor walks back to med and his patient. Pell stands his watch in a room that is not his.*

*End card. Next: eight.*
