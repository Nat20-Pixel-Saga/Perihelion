Sixty-three empty cabins. One light still on.

The Perihelion comes alongside the lost ship. Her lights are on, her beacon counts one, and somebody answers a signal lamp. The captain goes across herself.

Episode 8, "Aphelion", airs Thursday 5 November on this channel.

PERIHELION is a science-fiction serial told entirely through a ship's console: the viewscreen, the star map, the signal strip, the deck plan, the captain's log and the comms. Watch the instruments. They show more than the log says.
