Sixty-three voices. One of them is her brother.

The Perihelion reaches the source of the signal. It is not a ship and it is not a star. Sixty-three voices are waiting there, and one of them knows the captain by her first name.

Episode 9, "The Register", airs Saturday 7 November on this channel.

PERIHELION is a science-fiction serial told entirely through a ship's console: the viewscreen, the star map, the signal strip, the deck plan, the captain's log and the comms. Watch the instruments. They show more than the log says.
