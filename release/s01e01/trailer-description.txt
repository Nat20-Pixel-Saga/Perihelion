The beacon should pulse every 60 seconds.

A survey ship reaches the first beacon left by a lost expedition. It pulses every 61 seconds instead of 60, and its memory explains why.

Episode 1, "Sixty-one seconds", airs Tuesday 20 October on this channel.

PERIHELION is a science-fiction serial told entirely through a ship's console: the viewscreen, the star map, the signal strip, the deck plan, the captain's log and the comms. Watch the instruments. They show more than the log says.
