Sixty-four people went out. None came back.

A survey ship follows a chain of beacons left by a lost expedition. Each beacon pulses at an interval equal to the number of people who were still aboard when it was set, and the number keeps falling.

PERIHELION is a science-fiction serial told entirely through a ship's console: the viewscreen, the star map, the signal strip, the deck plan, the captain's log and the comms. Watch the instruments. They show more than the log says.

Season 1: 10 episodes, starting Tuesday 20 October. New episodes Tue, Thu, Sat at 18:00 (Paris time).

Music and sound: original score generated by the Perihelion pipeline.
