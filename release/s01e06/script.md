# The seventh

Perihelion · episode 6 · 7:24

The first officer calls a vote of confidence in the captain. Before anyone walks, she has to account for one night, and for eight tenths of a second.

## Scene 1 · Article nine (0:04)

*Open on the deck plan. Six dots in the mess, one in medical. The bridge, the lab and the drive room are empty. The relay pulses every eight seconds.*

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 432 · 07:55**

> All hands in the mess at 08:00, at Mr Okafor's request. The bridge is on automatic. I know what he is going to ask. I have not asked him to wait.

**Okafor:** All hands. Madrigal, do you have us in medical?

**Madrigal:** I have you. And a view of the ceiling.

**Okafor:** Under article nine I am calling a vote of confidence in the captain.

**Castellanos:** Article nine needs a second.

**Pell:** Seconded.

**Okafor:** Before anyone votes. Captain, account for 02:14 on day 414.

**Varga:** You are entitled to that. All of you.

*The captain gives her account standing. She does not raise her voice and she does not hurry.*

**Varga:** My brother is Dominik Varga. Hydrologist, DSV Aphelion.

**Halloran:** You never said you had anyone aboard her.

**Varga:** No. I did not.

**Varga:** Beacon 1 said three were gone. It did not say which three.

**Varga:** At 02:14 I sent a roster query through the relay. Eight tenths of a second.

**Varga:** I asked a machine if my brother was alive.

**Pell:** Did it answer?

**Varga:** The beacon did not. Something else did.

*Nobody speaks. On the signal strip the teal trace crosses once, and eight seconds later crosses again. It is the answer she got.*

**Achterberg:** Did it tell you if he is among them?

**Varga:** It told me seven. Nothing else.

**Castellanos:** And the log.

**Varga:** I logged that I was asleep in my quarters. That was a lie.

**Varga:** Mr Pell. Ms Castellanos. I wrote that you were beside the relay. Then I erased it and let the doubt stand.

**Pell:** You held an inquiry. You sat at the head of this table.

**Varga:** I did.

**Varga:** Then I let it rest on Mr Okafor. Eighteen days. He knew the truth and he carried it for me.

**Okafor:** I chose to. I should not have been asked to.

**Halloran:** Why not tell us the first morning?

**Varga:** I had given the order not to transmit. I broke it in six hours.

**Varga:** That is the account. I have no defence to add to it.

**Okafor:** Then it is on record.

## Scene 2 · Madrigal's list (2:23)

`day 432 · 08:40`

*Madrigal speaks from her bed in medical. Her dot has not been in the drive room for six days. Nobody else is in it either.*

**Madrigal:** Before anyone walks anywhere. I have a list.

**Okafor:** Go ahead, Madrigal.

*The viewscreen switches to the drive room log. Her objections print as she reads them, each with its date.*

**ON SCREEN · day 412 · approach to Tau-9**

> Engineer requests slower approach. Granted.

**Madrigal:** Day 412. I asked for a slower approach. She granted it.

**ON SCREEN · day 413 · close orbit, Tau-9 c**

> Cost seven points of fuel. Objection noted. Overruled.

**Madrigal:** Day 413. Seven points of fuel. Noted. We read the beacon.

**ON SCREEN · day 420 · debris, the Shallows**

> Cost three points of fuel. Objection noted. Overruled.

**Madrigal:** Day 420. The debris. Noted. We found the shuttle.

**ON SCREEN · day 426 · blind skim, Kiln**

> Flown on drive pressure alone. Objection noted, twice. Overruled.

**Madrigal:** Day 426. The blind skim. Noted twice. The tanks are full.

**Halloran:** And you are in my medical bay.

**Madrigal:** The tanks are full, Doctor.

*Hold on the four entries. Three of them end in the same word.*

**Madrigal:** Four notes. She was right to overrule me three times. I want that noted as well.

**Varga:** Noted.

**Pell:** Nobody says she cannot command the ship.

**Castellanos:** We say she lied to it.

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 432 · 08:55**

> Madrigal has read her objections into the record from the medical bay. Four. I had remembered three.

## Scene 3 · The vote (3:35)

`day 432 · 09:10`

**Okafor:** The vote is by standing. To retain, stay in the mess. To relieve, walk to cargo.

**Halloran:** Why cargo?

**Okafor:** It is where the relay is.

**Varga:** I do not vote. I will be on the bridge. Somebody should be.

*The captain walks out of the mess alone and takes the empty bridge. Five dots are left to decide. The sixth is in medical.*

**Okafor:** Relieve. I kept her silence for eighteen days. It was never mine to keep.

**Pell:** Relieve. She opened an inquiry into herself and let me sit in it.

*Two dots in cargo, beside the relay. Three in the mess. Nobody has yet said the word retain.*

**Achterberg:** Retain. I have wanted to answer it since Tau-9. She only did it first.

**Halloran:** Retain. I know what a person will do to hear one voice again.

*Castellanos has not moved. She looks at the two rooms the way she looks at a shelf.*

**Castellanos:** Two and two. I counted.

**Castellanos:** Relieve. I can forgive the query. I cannot count on a log that changes.

*Hold on the deck plan. Three dots in cargo, two in the mess, one on the bridge, one in medical. No dialogue.*

**Okafor:** Three to relieve. Two to retain. Madrigal, you are the last.

**Madrigal:** I cannot walk to either room.

**Okafor:** Your word will do.

**Madrigal:** Retain. On one condition.

**Pell:** That is three and three.

**Okafor:** Article nine. A tie keeps the captain.

**Castellanos:** The condition, Madrigal.

**Madrigal:** The log is open. Every console, every hand. And nothing in it is erased again.

**Madrigal:** If she writes it, it stays written. Note that.

**Okafor:** Captain. Do you accept?

**Varga:** I accept. Open it.

*Meter: morale 22% to 30%.*

*The log panel gains a line it will carry for the rest of the season: open, no erase.*

**Achterberg:** It reads what we delete. Now there is nothing to read.

## Scene 4 · Open log (5:58)

`day 432 · 10:30`

*The tags come off the rooms. The crew go back to work. Okafor stays in cargo a while, beside the relay.*

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 432 · 10:30**

> I sent the transmission. My brother is Dominik Varga. I do not know if he is alive. 

*The cursor waits at the end of the sentence. This is where she used to erase. She goes on.*

> I have stopped pretending I am not looking for him.

*Every console on the ship shows the entry as she types it. The last time that happened, the text was teal. No teal text answers now.*

**Achterberg:** It is still eight.

**Varga:** Let it count.

`day 432 · 15:20`

**Castellanos:** Frame 12 is patched. Pell held the plate. I counted the welds.

*Meter: hull 67% to 69%.*

*Okafor leaves cargo and walks the length of the ship to the bridge. He stops beside the captain, where he always stands.*

**Okafor:** Course, Captain?

**Varga:** Vesper.

*Meter: fuel 74% to 71%.*

**Varga:** You voted to relieve me, Mr Okafor.

**Okafor:** I did. It is in the log.

**Varga:** Good.

*Meter: morale 30% to 41%.*

**CAPTAIN'S LOG · day 432 · 21:50**

> Article nine: three to retain, three to relieve. I keep the command by a tie and a condition. Both are fair. Mr Okafor has the watch.

`day 432 · 22:05`

*At 22:05 the doctor walks to the mess, alone. The relay keeps its count of eight. Nothing on the ship is hidden tonight except what he carries on the second tray.*

*End card. Next: what we deleted.*
