Seven people aboard. The signal counts eight.

Three days out of Kiln, the pulse from the cargo relay slows from seven seconds to eight. There are seven people aboard. The crew search the ship for the eighth and look in the wrong place.

Episode 5, "Eight", airs Thursday 29 October on this channel.

PERIHELION is a science-fiction serial told entirely through a ship's console: the viewscreen, the star map, the signal strip, the deck plan, the captain's log and the comms. Watch the instruments. They show more than the log says.
